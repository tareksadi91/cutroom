— i The cut room

The cut lives in one JSON file under ~/cutroom-projects/, not in this page. Every change saves straight to it, and Claude edits the same file. Neither of you has to describe the cut to the other.

Output is 720×1280 at 24 fps, crf 10. Sources of another size are fitted and padded, never stretched.

cutroom never writes to, moves or deletes your media. It reads. A pass writes a new file into the project's own derived/ folder and re-points the clip at it.

i Lanes are a workspace, not layers

Nothing is ever composited over anything. Lanes just let you keep a section separate, or park an alternate take beside the shot it might replace.

Two clips blend when they overlap in time, in one lane or across lanes, it makes no difference. The renderer sorts by time and ignores the lane entirely.

Drag a clip up or down to move it. Drop it one row past the last lane to make a new one.
